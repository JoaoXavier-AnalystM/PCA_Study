1. Quick Review - The 20% That Delivers 80% of the Points

PromQL (28% of the exam)

rate(counter[5m])      # rate/s of the counter
irate(counter[5m])     # instantaneous rate (2 points)
increase(counter[1h])  # total over the period
delta(gauge[10m])      # gauge difference

histogram_quantile(0.99,
  sum by (le) (rate(bucket[5m]))
)  # P99 - NEVER forget the "le"

absent(up{job="api"})  # 1 if metric is missing

predict_linear(disk[1h], 4*3600) < 0
# alert if disk fills within 4h

sum by (job) (metric)      # keeps only job
sum without (pod) (metric) # removes pod

Metric Types

Counter   only goes up → use rate()
Gauge     goes up and down → use delta()
Histogram _bucket, _sum, _count
Summary   quantiles on the client

🎯 Alert States

inactive
Condition not met
pending
Condition OK but within the "for:" window
firing
Condition OK for longer than "for:" → sent to Alertmanager

⏱️ Alertmanager Timing

group_wait
Wait before the 1st notification of a new group (default: 30s)
group_interval
Interval between updates of the same group (default: 5m)
repeat_interval
Repeat an unresolved alert (default: 4h)

2. Technical Checklist

🚨 Do this BEFORE the exam - don't leave it to the last minute

Equipment

  • Webcam working and positioned correctly (shows face and hands)
  • Microphone tested - clear audio with no noise
  • Stable internet - test the speed (5Mbps+ recommended)
  • Laptop battery charged OR power cable connected
  • Updated browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended)
  • Close ALL tabs except the exam one
  • Disable system notifications (Slack, email, etc)

Physical Environment

  • Desk completely clear - ONLY water and an ID document
  • Wall behind you without posters/papers (proctor requirement)
  • Tell family/roommates NOT to interrupt for 2 hours
  • Silence your phone and keep it away from the desk
  • Good lighting - face visible on the webcam
  • Door closed, quiet environment

Documents

  • Photo ID (passport, CNH or RG) ready on the desk
  • Confirm the name on the ID matches the registration name
  • Check in 15min before the scheduled time

3. Strategy During the Exam

⏱️ Time Management

  • 90 minutes ÷ 60 questions = 90 seconds per question
  • Flag uncertain questions and move on
  • Keep the last 15min to review the flagged ones
  • Don't get stuck on a question - if you don't know it, flag and skip
  • Read the ENTIRE question before choosing an answer

🎯 Prioritization

  • Answer the easy ones first (confidence + guaranteed points)
  • PromQL questions are usually more straightforward
  • Conceptual questions (Observability, Alerting) can have traps
  • If between 2 options, go with your first intuition

Mindset

  • Breathe deeply if you feel anxious
  • Remember: you studied for 6 days, you are prepared
  • Don't compare yourself to others - focus on your exam
  • Trust your preparation - don't change answers on a hunch
  • You don't need to get them all right - 75% already passes

⚠️ Common Traps

  • Look for "NOT" or "EXCEPT" in the question
  • Mixing up rate() with irate()
  • Forgetting "le" in histogram_quantile
  • Swapping by() and without()
  • Mixing up delta() (gauge) with increase() (counter)
  • Getting the ports wrong (9090, 9091, 9093, 9100)
